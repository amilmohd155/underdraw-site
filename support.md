## Frequently asked questions

**The camera or my photos won't open.**
Underdraw needs your permission to use the camera and to pick photos. Open your phone's Settings, find Underdraw, and turn on Camera and Photos. You can still use the built-in templates in sketch mode without any permissions.

**How do I get my recent images back, or delete them?**
The app keeps your last 10 images inside the app on your phone. Older ones are removed automatically. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it saved.

**Does Underdraw need an internet connection?**
No. It works offline. The only internet use is a small check for app updates when it starts.

**The app is stuck or behaving strangely.**
Close the app completely and reopen it. If that doesn't help, update to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play, then try again.
